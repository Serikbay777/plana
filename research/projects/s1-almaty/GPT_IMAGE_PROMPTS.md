## Usage Guide

1. **Copy the prompt** for a specific sheet completely
2. Paste into GPT-IMAGE 2 / DALL-E 3 / Midjourney (for MJ add `--ar 16:9 --v 6`)
3. For **facades and interiors** add `--style raw` (MJ) or `realistic photo` for more photographic results
4. For **plans and nodes** keep as is — isometric/cutaway works best
